Connect your Gmail account

Used to show your unread inbox in the dashboard and let you mark messages read.

Not your normal password. Enable 2-Step Verification, then generate one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords — paste the 16 characters here (spaces are fine).

Connect Google Calendar

Lets the dashboard show upcoming events and add new ones for you.

From a free Google Cloud project: enable the Calendar API, configure the OAuth consent screen (External, Testing, add yourself as a test user), then create an OAuth Client ID of type "Desktop app" and paste its ID + secret here.

Sync across devices

Keeps to-dos, habits, and conferences in sync between this app and the web version, via your own Google Drive — uses the same Google connection as Calendar above.
